# Spanish Daily Lesson 26: Pronominal Verbs Inside Core Patterns

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Treating `-se` as optional or forgetting to change it for the subject.

**Spanish logic**  
With familiar frames (`tener que`, `querer`, `ir a`, `poder`), keep the second verb in infinitive and attach the correct reflexive pronoun: `-me, -te, -se, -nos, -se`.  
For body parts, use definite articles (`las manos`, `los dientes`), not possessives.

**target pattern / formula**  
- `tener que + [pronominal infinitive]` -> `Tengo que acostarme`  
- `querer + [pronominal infinitive]` -> `Quiero despertarme`  
- `ir a + [pronominal infinitive]` -> `Voy a lavarme`  
- `poder + [pronominal infinitive]` -> `Puedo sentarme`  
- `quejarse de + noun` -> `Quiero quejarme del ruido`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Tengo que levantarme temprano.  
2. Quieres sentarte aqui?  
3. Vamos a banarnos antes de salir.  
4. Puedo lavarme las manos?  
5. Quiero quejarme del servicio.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Tengo que acostarme temprano. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
2. Base: Tienes que levantarte a las seis. -> change subject to `yo`.  
3. Base: Quiere despertarse tarde. -> change subject to `ellos`.  
4. Base: Vamos a banarnos antes de cenar. -> change to singular `yo`.  
5. Base: Puedo sentarme aqui. -> change subject to `usted`.  
6. Base: Queremos lavarnos las manos. -> change to question with `ustedes`.  
7. Base: Van a levantarse manana. -> change to `hoy`.  
8. Base: Tiene que cepillarse los dientes. -> make it negative.  
9. Base: Voy a lavarme la cara. -> change object to `las manos`.  
10. Base: Quiero quejarme del ruido. -> change noun to `trafico`.  
11. Base: Se queja de todo. -> change to `nosotros`.  
12. Base: Nos quejamos del examen. -> change to `ella`.  
13. Base: Podemos sentarnos aqui. -> change to singular `yo` question.  
14. Base: Tienen que acostarse temprano. -> change to `tu`.  
15. Base: Va a banarse. -> change to `querer` pattern with `yo`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. I have to get up early. (`Tengo que levantarme temprano.` vs `Tengo que me levantar temprano.`)  
2. You want to sit here. (`Quieres sentarte aqui.` vs `Quieres te sentar aqui.`)  
3. We are going to wash our hands. (`Vamos a lavarnos las manos.` vs `Vamos a lavarnos nuestras manos.` as default pattern)  
4. Can I wash my face? (`Puedo lavarme la cara?` vs `Puedo lavar me la cara?`)  
5. They have to brush their teeth. (`Tienen que cepillarse los dientes.` vs `Tienen que cepillar se los dientes.`)  
6. I want to complain about the service. (`Quiero quejarme del servicio.` vs `Quiero quejarme de el servicio.`)  
7. She complains about everything. (`Se queja de todo.` vs `Queja de todo.`)  
8. We can sit down now. (`Podemos sentarnos ahora.` vs `Podemos nos sentar ahora.`)  
9. You all are going to get up late. (`Van a levantarse tarde.` vs `Van a se levantar tarde.`)  
10. I am going to wash my hands. (`Voy a lavarme las manos.` vs `Voy a lavarme mis manos.` as neutral pattern)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Tengo que levantar temprano.`  
   **Why it happens:** Dropping reflexive part from infinitive.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Tengo que levantarme temprano.`

2. **Mistake:** `Quiero te sentar.`  
   **Why it happens:** Keeping pronoun separate in wrong position.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Quiero sentarte.`

3. **Mistake:** `Vamos a nos banar.`  
   **Why it happens:** Pronoun inserted before infinitive incorrectly in this pattern.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Vamos a banarnos.`

4. **Mistake:** `Puedo lavarme mis manos.`  
   **Why it happens:** English possessive transfer with body parts.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Puedo lavarme las manos.`

5. **Mistake:** `Tienes que cepillarte tu dientes.`  
   **Why it happens:** Overusing possessive adjectives with body parts.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Tienes que cepillarte los dientes.`

6. **Mistake:** `Quiero quejarme de el ruido.`  
   **Why it happens:** Missing contraction with `de + el`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Quiero quejarme del ruido.`

7. **Mistake:** `Queja de todo.`  
   **Why it happens:** Forgetting required pronoun in pronominal verb.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se queja de todo.`

8. **Mistake:** `Podemos nos sentar aqui.`  
   **Why it happens:** Wrong placement for reflexive pronoun in infinitive chunk.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Podemos sentarnos aqui.`

9. **Mistake:** `Van a se levantar.`  
   **Why it happens:** Subject pronoun confusion with reflexive pronoun.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Van a levantarse.`

10. **Mistake:** Treating `quejarse` as optional `de` verb  
    **Why it happens:** Not learning verb with required preposition.  
    **Correct Spanish:** Learn as chunk `quejarse de`.
