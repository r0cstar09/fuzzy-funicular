# Spanish Daily Lesson 25: Pronominal Verbs by Function

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Treating every `se` verb as the same and translating word-for-word from English pronoun logic.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish uses pronominal forms for different functions. You choose the pattern by meaning, not by memorizing one English equivalent for `se`.

**target pattern / formula**  
- true reflexive: pronoun + verb + definite article body part (`me lavo las manos`)  
- reciprocal: plural subject + `se` + plural verb (`nos ayudamos`)  
- inherently pronominal chunk: `quejarse de`, `arrepentirse de`  
- passive `se`: `se` + third-person verb agreeing with noun (`se venden boletos`)  
- meaning shift pairs: `dormir` vs `dormirse`, `ir` vs `irse`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Me lavo la cara antes de salir.  
2. Nos ayudamos cuando hay mucho trabajo.  
3. Me quejo del ruido del trafico.  
4. Se venden boletos en linea.  
5. Me dormi en el sofa y luego me fui tarde.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as directed.

1. Base: Me lavo las manos. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
2. Base: Se cepilla los dientes. -> change subject to `ustedes`.  
3. Base: Nos abrazamos. -> change to past (preterite).  
4. Base: Se ayudan en clase. -> make it negative.  
5. Base: Me quejo del ruido. -> change subject to `ella`.  
6. Base: Nos arrepentimos del error. -> change to singular `yo` in preterite.  
7. Base: Se venden boletos. -> change noun to singular `la entrada`.  
8. Base: Se habla espanol aqui. -> change location to `en mi empresa`.  
9. Base: Duermo ocho horas. -> change to fall asleep on the sofa.  
10. Base: Me duermo tarde. -> change to `nosotros` and add `casi siempre`.  
11. Base: Vamos al centro. -> change meaning to we leave now.  
12. Base: Se van temprano. -> change to question with `ustedes`.  
13. Base: Ellos se llaman por telefono. -> change to `nosotros` in present progressive.  
14. Base: Me enojo facilmente. -> change to question with `tu`.  
15. Base: Voy a sentarme aqui. -> change to can I sit here?

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Lavo mis manos before eating`  
   **Why it happens:** Direct transfer of English possessive body-part pattern.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Me lavo las manos antes de comer.`

2. **Mistake:** `Nosotros abrazamos` when meaning each other  
   **Why it happens:** Reciprocal `se` is omitted.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Nosotros nos abrazamos.`

3. **Mistake:** `Quejo del ruido`  
   **Why it happens:** Inherently pronominal verb is treated as non-pronominal.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Me quejo del ruido.`

4. **Mistake:** `Se arrepiente la decision`  
   **Why it happens:** Required preposition `de` is missing.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se arrepiente de la decision.`

5. **Mistake:** `Se vende boletos`  
   **Why it happens:** Verb agreement with plural noun is ignored.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se venden boletos.`

6. **Mistake:** `Me duermo ocho horas`  
   **Why it happens:** `Dormir` and `dormirse` meanings are mixed.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Duermo ocho horas.`

7. **Mistake:** `Voy ahora` for I am leaving now  
   **Why it happens:** `Ir` and `irse` meaning contrast not applied.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Me voy ahora.`

8. **Mistake:** `Se van a la oficina a las ocho` used for we go there habitually  
   **Why it happens:** Leaving and going are confused.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Vamos a la oficina a las ocho.`

9. **Mistake:** `Ustedes llaman cada semana` when meaning each other  
   **Why it happens:** Reciprocal action is expressed without pronoun.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Ustedes se llaman cada semana.`

10. **Mistake:** `Puedo sentar aqui?`  
    **Why it happens:** Reflexive infinitive is dropped in set chunk.  
    **Correct Spanish:** `Puedo sentarme aqui?`
