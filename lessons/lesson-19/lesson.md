# Spanish Daily Lesson 19: Ownership Without Word-for-Word Translation

## 1) Cognitive Shift

**English trap:**  
Thinking in English ownership order ("my, your, his") and forcing literal structures like "of who is."

**Spanish logic:**  
Spanish asks ownership with `¿De quién es/son ...?`, uses short possessive adjectives before nouns (`mi`, `tu`, `su`), and uses article + possessive pronoun when replacing nouns (`el mío`, `la tuya`, `los suyos`).

**Target pattern / formula:**  
- `¿De quién es + singular noun?`
- `¿De quién son + plural noun?`
- `No, no es mío / mía. Es de + name.`
- `No, no son míos / mías. Son de + name.`
- `su/suyo` may need clarification: `de él / de ella / de ellos`

**5 natural examples:**  
1. ¿De quién es este cargador?  
2. ¿De quién son estos documentos?  
3. No, no es mío. Es de Laura.  
4. No, no son mías. Son de mi jefe.  
5. ¿Es suyo o de ella?

## 3) Pattern Mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: ¿De quién es el portátil? -> change noun to plural (`los portátiles`).  
2. Base: ¿De quién son las llaves? -> change noun to singular (`la llave`).  
3. Base: No, no es mío. -> change speaker to feminine.  
4. Base: No, no son mías. -> change possessor to masculine plural.  
5. Base: Es de Carlos. -> replace with possessive pronoun (`suyo`).  
6. Base: Son de Ana. -> replace with possessive pronoun (`suyas`).  
7. Base: ¿Es tuyo? -> make it plural feminine.  
8. Base: ¿Son tuyas? -> make it singular masculine.  
9. Base: Es su computadora. -> clarify owner is "her" using `de`.  
10. Base: Son sus documentos. -> clarify owner is "them" using `de`.  
11. Base: Es mi abrigo. -> replace noun with possessive pronoun.  
12. Base: Son tus gafas. -> replace noun with possessive pronoun.  
13. Base: ¿De quién es la silla? -> ask about `los asientos`.  
14. Base: No, no es suyo. -> change to first person plural ownership.  
15. Base: Son nuestras llaves. -> rewrite with pronoun-only answer.

## 4) Contrastive Discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. Whose laptop is it? (`¿De quién es el portátil?` vs `¿Quién de es el portátil?`)  
2. Whose reports are they? (`¿De quién son los informes?` vs `¿De quién es los informes?`)  
3. It's mine. (`Es mío.` vs `Es mi.`)  
4. They are mine. (`Son míos.` vs `Son mi.`)  
5. Is it yours? (`¿Es tuyo?` vs `¿Es tu?`)  
6. Are they yours? (`¿Son tuyas?` vs `¿Son tuyasas?`)  
7. It's her office. (`Es su oficina.` vs `Es la oficina su.`)  
8. It's hers. (`Es suya.` vs `Es ella.`)  
9. They are theirs. (`Son suyos.` vs `Son de su.`)  
10. It's his car. (`Es su coche.` vs `Es coche suyo` as neutral default sentence)

## 7) Common Errors

1. **Mistake:** `¿Quién es el portátil?`  
   **Why it happens:** Literal transfer from English "who is".  
   **Correct Spanish:** `¿De quién es el portátil?`

2. **Mistake:** `¿De quién es las llaves?`  
   **Why it happens:** Forgetting number agreement with `es/son`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `¿De quién son las llaves?`

3. **Mistake:** `Es mi` (when noun is omitted)  
   **Why it happens:** Confusing adjective and pronoun forms.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Es mío / mía.`

4. **Mistake:** `Son mi`  
   **Why it happens:** Not switching to plural pronoun form.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Son míos / mías.`

5. **Mistake:** `Es de ella casa`  
   **Why it happens:** Mixing two ownership structures at once.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Es su casa.` or `Es la casa de ella.`

6. **Mistake:** `¿Es tu?`  
   **Why it happens:** Using adjective form instead of pronoun form.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `¿Es tuyo / tuya?`

7. **Mistake:** `el mío coche`  
   **Why it happens:** Combining pronoun phrase with a noun incorrectly.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `mi coche` or `el mío` (without noun).

8. **Mistake:** Overusing possessives with body/clothing (`mi cabeza`, `mi camisa` in obvious context)  
   **Why it happens:** English influence.  
   **Correct Spanish:** Often article preferred: `Me duele la cabeza.`, `Ponte la camisa.`

9. **Mistake:** `su` always interpreted as "his"  
   **Why it happens:** `su/sus` are ambiguous.  
   **Correct Spanish:** Clarify with `de él / de ella / de ellos` when needed.

10. **Mistake:** Gender mismatch in pronouns (`la mío`, `los mía`)  
    **Why it happens:** Learner tracks owner, not noun gender/number.  
    **Correct Spanish:** Match replaced noun: `la mía`, `los míos`, `las suyas`.
