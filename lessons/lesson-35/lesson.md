# Spanish Daily Lesson 35: Plug-and-Play Starters + Infinitive

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Trying to fully conjugate two verbs in one sentence.

**Spanish logic**  
In these high-frequency frames, only the first verb is conjugated. The second verb stays in infinitive form. With pronominal verbs, switch `-se` to `me` (yo) or `te` (tu) and attach it to the infinitive.

**target pattern / formula**  
- `quiero + infinitive`  
- `tengo que + infinitive`  
- `puedo + infinitive`  
- `deberia / deberias + infinitive`  
- `podria / podrias + infinitive`  
- `voy a / vas a + infinitive`  
- `acabo de / acabas de + infinitive`  
- pronominal: `acostarse -> acostarme / acostarte`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Quiero terminar este informe.  
2. Tengo que llamar al cliente.  
3. Puedo salir mas tarde.  
4. Voy a acostarme temprano.  
5. Acabas de levantarte.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Quiero trabajar hoy. -> change subject to tu.  
2. Base: Tengo que estudiar ahora. -> change to question form with yo and `puedo`.  
3. Base: Puedo salir temprano. -> make it negative.  
4. Base: Voy a confirmar la informacion. -> change time to manana.  
5. Base: Acabo de cancelar la reserva. -> change subject to tu.  
6. Base: Deberias acostarte temprano. -> change advice verb to `podrias`.  
7. Base: Quiero levantarme a las seis. -> change subject to tu.  
8. Base: Tienes que despertarte ahora. -> change to yo form.  
9. Base: Puedo ir a la tienda. -> change destination to el banco.  
10. Base: Voy a acostarme temprano. -> change to question with tu.  
11. Base: Acabas de levantarte. -> change to yo form.  
12. Base: Deberia llamar al cliente. -> change to past-recent frame with `acabo de`.  
13. Base: Podrias revisar el documento. -> change object to plural `los documentos`.  
14. Base: Quieres colaborar con el equipo? -> make it a statement in yo form.  
15. Base: Tengo que ir a la oficina. -> change to future intention with `voy a`.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Quiero trabajo.`  
   **Why it happens:** English transfer after "want."  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Quiero trabajar.`

2. **Mistake:** `Tengo que estudio.`  
   **Why it happens:** Conjugating the second verb.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Tengo que estudiar.`

3. **Mistake:** `Puedo salgo?`  
   **Why it happens:** Double conjugation in modal frame.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Puedo salir?`

4. **Mistake:** `Voy descanso.`  
   **Why it happens:** Missing `a + infinitive` after `voy`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Voy a descansar.`

5. **Mistake:** `Acabo levantarme.`  
   **Why it happens:** Missing `de` in recent-past frame.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Acabo de levantarme.`

6. **Mistake:** `Quiero me acostar.`  
   **Why it happens:** Reflexive pronoun not attached to infinitive.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Quiero acostarme.`

7. **Mistake:** `Deberias te acostar temprano.`  
   **Why it happens:** Wrong pronoun placement with infinitive.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Deberias acostarte temprano.`

8. **Mistake:** `Podrias me ayudar.`  
   **Why it happens:** Object pronoun in unnatural position.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Podrias ayudarme.`

9. **Mistake:** `Tienes que levantarse.`  
   **Why it happens:** Keeping dictionary `-se` with tu subject.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Tienes que levantarte.`

10. **Mistake:** `Tengo que levantarte.` (when meaning I get up)  
    **Why it happens:** Confusing `me` and `te`.  
    **Correct Spanish:** `Tengo que levantarme.`
