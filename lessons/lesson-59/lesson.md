# Spanish Daily Lesson 59: Verb + Preposition Chunks

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Choosing Spanish prepositions by translating from English word for word.

**Spanish logic**  
In Spanish, many verbs come as fixed chunks with specific prepositions.  
The preposition is part of the pattern, and changing it can change meaning.  
Train your brain on full chunks, not isolated verbs.

**target pattern / formula**  
- `soñar con + noun/infinitive`
- `pensar + infinitive` (plan), `pensar en` (think about), `pensar de` (opinion)
- reflexive chunks: `enamorarse de`, `despedirse de`, `quejarse de`, `burlarse de`, `cansarse de`
- action chunks: `invitar a`, `aprender a`, `ensenar a`, `empezar a`, `ayudar a`
- support/dependence chunks: `contar con`, `depender de`, `acostumbrarse a`, `abusar de`, `limitarse a`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Sueno con vivir cerca del mar.  
2. Pienso mudarme el proximo ano.  
3. Siempre pienso en mis padres.  
4. Que piensas del nuevo supervisor?  
5. Puedes contar conmigo.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Sueno con vivir en la playa. -> change location to `la montana`.  
2. Base: Pensamos mudarnos a Mexico. -> change subject to singular `yo`.  
3. Base: Siempre pienso en ti. -> change pronoun to plural `ustedes`.  
4. Base: Que piensas del nuevo supervisor? -> change noun to `nuevo horario`.  
5. Base: Maria se enamoro de Julio. -> change object to `su mejor amigo`.  
6. Base: Mi primo se despidio de nosotros. -> change pronoun to singular `mi`.  
7. Base: Juan se canso de trabajar. -> change infinitive to `esperar`.  
8. Base: Los estudiantes se burlan del profesor. -> change to singular subject `el estudiante`.  
9. Base: Me limito a beber dos copas. -> change infinitive to `comer postre`.  
10. Base: Mi primo me invito a cenar. -> change infinitive to `almorzar`.  
11. Base: Aprendi a nadar a los cinco anos. -> change skill to `conducir`.  
12. Base: Mi padre me enseno a cocinar. -> change teacher to `mi madre` and skill `coser`.  
13. Base: Vamos a empezar a pintar la casa. -> change verb `pintar` to `limpiar`.  
14. Base: Siempre puedes contar conmigo. -> change pronoun to plural `nosotros`.  
15. Base: La duracion del viaje depende del trafico. -> change noun phrase to `el costo`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct option.

1. I dream of being a lawyer. (`Sueno con ser abogado.` vs `Sueno de ser abogado.`)  
2. We plan to travel. (`Pensamos viajar.` vs `Pensamos en viajar.`)  
3. I think about my family. (`Pienso en mi familia.` vs `Pienso de mi familia.`)  
4. What do you think of the plan? (`Que piensas del plan?` vs `Que piensas en el plan?`)  
5. She fell in love with him. (`Se enamoro de el.` vs `Se enamoro con el.`)  
6. He said goodbye to me. (`Se despidio de mi.` vs `Se despidio con mi.`)  
7. We started to study. (`Empezamos a estudiar.` vs `Empezamos de estudiar.`)  
8. Can I count on you? (`Puedo contar contigo?` vs `Puedo contar en ti?`)  
9. It depends on the weather. (`Depende del clima.` vs `Depende en el clima.`)  
10. She complains about everything. (`Se queja de todo.` vs `Se queja con todo.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** translating preposition directly from English  
   **Why it happens:** learners map one-to-one across languages.  
   **Correct Spanish:** memorize verb + preposition as one unit.

2. **Mistake:** using `sonar de` instead of `sonar con`  
   **Why it happens:** "dream of" interference.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use `sonar con`.

3. **Mistake:** using `pensar en` when expressing plans  
   **Why it happens:** not distinguishing meanings.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use `pensar + infinitive` for plans.

4. **Mistake:** using `pensar de` for "think about"  
   **Why it happens:** mixing opinion vs mental focus patterns.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `pensar en` means think about.

5. **Mistake:** forgetting reflexive pronoun in pronominal verbs  
   **Why it happens:** verb chunk learned incompletely.  
   **Correct Spanish:** keep forms like `quejarse de`, `enamorarse de`.

6. **Mistake:** wrong preposition with `contar` meaning rely on  
   **Why it happens:** confusion with `pensar en` type patterns.  
   **Correct Spanish:** rely on someone is `contar con`.

7. **Mistake:** using gerund after preposition where infinitive is required  
   **Why it happens:** direct translation from English -ing forms.  
   **Correct Spanish:** Spanish prepositions take infinitive.

8. **Mistake:** dropping contraction in `del` patterns  
   **Why it happens:** ignoring article after `de`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use `del` in forms like `pensar del`, `depender del`.

9. **Mistake:** mixing `despedirse de` with non-pronominal meanings  
   **Why it happens:** same root verb has multiple meanings.  
   **Correct Spanish:** farewell pattern is reflexive `despedirse de`.

10. **Mistake:** learning long verb lists without context  
    **Why it happens:** isolated memorization strategy.  
    **Correct Spanish:** practice chunks in full adult-life sentences.
