# Spanish Daily Lesson 10 (Writing-First)

## 1) cognitive_shift

- **English trap:** Using one fixed “this/that” form without gender/number matching.
- **Spanish logic:** Demonstratives must agree with the noun: masculine/feminine + singular/plural. Use neuter (`esto/eso`) for ideas or unknown things.
- **target pattern/formula:**
  - `este/esta/estos/estas + noun` (this/these)
  - `ese/esa/esos/esas + noun` (that/those)
  - `aquel/aquella/aquellos/aquellas + noun` (that/those over there)

### 5 natural examples
1. Quiero esta mesa.
2. No me gusta ese carro.
3. ¿Ves aquellos edificios?
4. ¿Qué es esto?
5. Quiero hablar de eso.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: `Este libro es interesante.` -> change to feminine singular (`revista`).
2. Base: `Esa silla es cómoda.` -> change to plural.
3. Base: `Aquella casa es antigua.` -> change to masculine singular (`edificio`).
4. Base: `Quiero este.` -> change noun reference to feminine (`camisa`).
5. Base: `No quiero esos.` -> change to singular masculine.
6. Base: `¿Quieres esta mesa?` -> change to pronoun form (“this one”).
7. Base: `¿Qué es esto?` -> change to “What is that?”
8. Base: `Quiero hablar de eso.` -> make it negative.
9. Base: `En ese entonces, trabajaba mucho.` -> change to `aquel entonces`.
10. Base: `Estos zapatos son caros.` -> change demonstrative to farther distance.
11. Base: `No quiero esa.` -> turn into question.
12. Base: `Aquellos niños están en el parque.` -> change to feminine plural (`niñas`).
13. Base: `Este teléfono funciona bien.` -> change to plural (`teléfonos`).
14. Base: `¿Quieres ese cuaderno?` -> answer yes using pronoun.
15. Base: `Esta puerta está cerrada.` -> change to “those doors.”

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the most natural option and write in Spanish.

1. “This book” (`este libro` vs `esta libro`)
2. “That chair” (`esa silla` vs `ese silla`)
3. “These windows” (`estas ventanas` vs `estos ventanas`)
4. “Those cars” (`esos carros` vs `esas carros`)
5. “That mountain over there” (`aquella montaña` vs `esa montaña`) emphasis on far distance.
6. “What is this?” (`¿qué es esto?` vs `¿qué es este?`) unknown object.
7. “I want this one.” (la mesa) (`quiero esta` vs `quiero este`)
8. “I don’t want those ones.” (los libros) (`no quiero esos` vs `no quiero esas`)
9. “At that time” (`en ese entonces` vs `en ese tiempo` in this lesson frame)
10. “Back then” with stronger distance (`en aquel entonces` vs `en ese entonces`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Wrong gender with demonstrative**  
   Why: English “this/that” has no gender.  
   Correct: `esta casa`, `ese carro`
2. **Wrong number agreement**  
   Why: Learner changes noun but not demonstrative.  
   Correct: `estos libros`, `esas sillas`
3. **Using noun-form demonstrative as neuter**  
   Why: Confusing `este` with `esto`.  
   Correct: `¿Qué es esto?`
4. **Using `esto/eso` before a noun**  
   Why: Neuter forms overgeneralized.  
   Correct: `este libro`, not `esto libro`
5. **Overusing `aquel` in every “that” context**  
   Why: Literal dictionary mapping.  
   Better usually: `ese/esa` for normal distance
6. **Dropping accent mark in `qué` questions**  
   Why: Accent omitted in fast writing.  
   Correct: `¿Qué es esto?`
7. **Mixing pronoun/adjective use**  
   Why: Not distinguishing with/without noun.  
   Correct: `Quiero esta camisa` / `Quiero esta`
8. **Wrong pronoun choice from noun gender**  
   Why: Reference noun not tracked mentally.  
   Correct: `(la mesa) quiero esta`
9. **Using direct English order with time phrase**  
   Why: Word-for-word transfer.  
   Correct: `En ese entonces...`
10. **Using `ese` when speaker means “this near me”**  
    Why: Distance logic not internalized.  
    Correct: `este` for near speaker objects
