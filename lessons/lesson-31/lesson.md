# Spanish Daily Lesson 31: Present Tense for Now, Habit, Near Future, and Polite Requests

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Overusing progressive forms and future/conditional forms where Spanish prefers simple present.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish present tense is highly flexible: it covers current actions, habits, near-future plans with time markers, and polite requests in everyday speech.

**target pattern / formula**  
- Right now / general now: `presente simple` (`Que haces?`)  
- Habit: `presente + frequency` (`siempre`, `todos los sabados`)  
- Near future: `presente + future time marker` (`manana`, `el domingo`)  
- Polite request: `Me + verbo en presente + complemento, por favor`  
- Motion verbs in present for current/near action: `voy`, `viene`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Trabajo desde casa hoy.  
2. Maria limpia la casa los sabados.  
3. Manana salgo temprano.  
4. Me pasas la sal, por favor?  
5. Voy al banco ahora.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Yo camino a la escuela todos los dias. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
2. Base: Ahora entiendo la respuesta. -> change subject to `ella`.  
3. Base: Manana salimos para el aeropuerto. -> change time marker to `hoy`.  
4. Base: Me ayudas con esto? -> change to formal `usted`.  
5. Base: Voy al banco. -> make it negative.  
6. Base: Me dices la hora, por favor? -> change to plural polite request (`ustedes`).  
7. Base: El siempre toma te por la noche. -> change adverb to `nunca` and keep present tense.  
8. Base: Visitas a tus abuelos este fin de semana? -> change to statement with `yo`.  
9. Base: Me pasas el cuaderno, por favor? -> change object to `la sal`.  
10. Base: Mis amigos vienen a mi casa despues de la escuela. -> change subject to singular `mi amigo`.  
11. Base: Nosotros cenamos a las siete todas las noches. -> change to question.  
12. Base: Te llamo manana. -> change direct object pronoun to `le` formal.  
13. Base: Empiezas manana tu nuevo trabajo? -> change to statement with `nosotros`.  
14. Base: Ella se levanta a las seis todos los dias. -> change to plural `ellas`.  
15. Base: Tenemos que salir temprano manana. -> change to `yo`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. I walk every day. (`Camino todos los dias.` vs `Estoy caminando todos los dias.`)  
2. Tomorrow we leave early. (`Manana salimos temprano.` vs `Manana saldremos temprano.` in this lesson target style)  
3. Can you help me? (`Me ayudas?` vs `Me estas ayudando?`)  
4. I am going to the store. (`Voy a la tienda.` vs `Estoy yendo a la tienda.` default conversational preference)  
5. What are you doing? (`Que haces?` vs `Que estas haciendo?` where simple present is preferred in this lesson)  
6. He comes to my house every Friday. (`Viene a mi casa cada viernes.` vs `Esta viniendo a mi casa cada viernes.`)  
7. Could you pass me the salt? (`Me pasas la sal?` vs `Me pasaras la sal?`)  
8. We eat at seven. (`Comemos a las siete.` vs `Estamos comiendo a las siete.` habitual meaning)  
9. Do you start tomorrow? (`Empiezas manana?` vs `Empezaras manana?` in this lesson target style)  
10. Can you lend me a pen? (`Me prestas una pluma?` vs `Me prestaras una pluma?`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** overusing progressive (`estoy caminando`) for habits  
   **Why it happens:** English transfer from present continuous.  
   **Correct Spanish:** habitual actions usually use simple present (`camino`).

2. **Mistake:** forcing future tense for all tomorrow references  
   **Why it happens:** English future bias.  
   **Correct Spanish:** near future with time marker often uses present (`manana salgo`).

3. **Mistake:** using rigid modal forms for polite requests only  
   **Why it happens:** Translating could/would literally.  
   **Correct Spanish:** natural request can be simple present (`me ayudas?`).

4. **Mistake:** `estoy yendo` with common motion verbs in basic conversation  
   **Why it happens:** direct mapping from "I am going."  
   **Correct Spanish:** `voy` is usually preferred.

5. **Mistake:** missing article with weekdays  
   **Why it happens:** English day expression transfer.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `el domingo`, `los sabados`.

6. **Mistake:** wrong formality level in request  
   **Why it happens:** not switching between `me pasas` and `me pasa/me ayuda`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** match `tu` vs `usted` context.

7. **Mistake:** mixing tense inside one timeline phrase  
   **Why it happens:** uncertainty between now/habit/future.  
   **Correct Spanish:** choose one clear frame with time marker.

8. **Mistake:** literal progressive in questions (`que estas esperando`) every time  
   **Why it happens:** over-literal translation.  
   **Correct Spanish:** simple form often sounds more natural (`que esperas`).

9. **Mistake:** ignoring pronoun placement in polite request chunks  
   **Why it happens:** focus on verb only.  
   **Correct Spanish:** keep chunk `me + verbo` (`me prestas`, `me trae`).

10. **Mistake:** memorizing conjugations without usage patterns  
    **Why it happens:** form-first learning only.  
    **Correct Spanish:** practice function contexts: now, habit, near future, polite request.
