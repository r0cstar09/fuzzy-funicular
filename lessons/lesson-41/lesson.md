# Spanish Daily Lesson 41: Preterite vs Imperfect in Real Stories

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Using one past form for every sentence and translating "was/went/knew/could" literally without context.

**Spanish logic**  
Use **preterite** for completed events that move the story forward. Use **imperfect** for background, repeated habits, ongoing states, and context. Some verbs change meaning by tense (`conocer`, `saber`, `querer`, `poder`, `tener`).

**target pattern / formula**  
- completed event -> preterite (`llegue`, `empezo`, `vi`)  
- background/habit/state -> imperfect (`era`, `iba`, `tenia`, `hablaba`)  
- ongoing action interrupted by event -> imperfect + preterite (`estaba leyendo cuando llamo`)  
- meaning-shift verbs:  
  - `conoci` (met) vs `conocia` (knew)  
  - `supe` (found out) vs `sabia` (knew)  
  - `quise/no quise` (wanted/refused) vs `queria` (wanted)  
  - `pude` (managed to) vs `podia` (was able/could)  
  - `tuve` (got/received) vs `tenia` (had)

**5 natural examples**  
1. Cuando era nino, iba a la playa cada verano.  
2. Ayer lei un libro interesante.  
3. Estabamos en el parque cuando empezo a llover.  
4. Conoci a Juan en 2010, pero Maria ya lo conocia.  
5. Yo podia trabajar el sabado, pero no pude ir.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Era nino cuando viviamos en Lima. -> change subject to nosotros for first clause.  
2. Base: Lei un libro ayer. -> change object to plural `dos articulos`.  
3. Base: Cocinabas cuando sono el telefono. -> change pronoun to `yo`.  
4. Base: Estabamos en el parque cuando empezo a llover. -> change location to `la playa`.  
5. Base: Juan estaba en el ejercito cuando conocio a Maria. -> change `conocio` to imperfect meaning.  
6. Base: Queria salir, pero no podia. -> change second verb to successful preterite result.  
7. Base: Llegue a la reunion y todos hablaban. -> change first verb to `llegaste`.  
8. Base: Mi papa fumaba todos los dias. -> make it negative habitual.  
9. Base: Visitamos a nuestros abuelos el domingo pasado. -> change subject to `ellas`.  
10. Base: Hablaba con su amigo cuando escucho un ruido. -> change subject to `nosotros` first verb.  
11. Base: No sabia que querias ir. -> change `sabia` to preterite find-out meaning.  
12. Base: Conoci a Laura en 2020. -> change to imperfect know meaning.  
13. Base: Pude terminar el informe. -> change to imperfect capability only.  
14. Base: Tuve la impresion de que no queria venir. -> change `tuve` to imperfect.  
15. Base: Hacia frio y el cielo parecia gris. -> add completed interruption: `entonces...`

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct option for the intended meaning.

1. I met Juan in 2010. (`Conoci a Juan en 2010.` vs `Conocia a Juan en 2010.`)  
2. I knew Juan. (`Conocia a Juan.` vs `Conoci a Juan.`)  
3. When did you find out? (`Cuando supiste?` vs `Cuando sabias?`)  
4. I did not know you wanted to go. (`No sabia que querias ir.` vs `No supe que querias ir.` for ongoing ignorance)  
5. I managed to finish. (`Pude terminar.` vs `Podia terminar.`)  
6. I was able to work Saturdays (in general). (`Podia trabajar los sabados.` vs `Pude trabajar los sabados.`)  
7. I got the impression... (`Tuve la impresion...` vs `Tenia la impresion...` for a specific moment)  
8. I had the impression... (`Tenia la impresion...` vs `Tuve la impresion...` for ongoing state)  
9. I wanted to scream at that moment. (`Quise gritar.` vs `Queria gritar.`)  
10. I refused to tell him. (`No quise decirle.` vs `No queria decirle.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** using preterite for repeated habits (`fume todos los dias`)  
   **Why it happens:** forgetting habitual framing.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `fumaba todos los dias`.

2. **Mistake:** using imperfect for one finished event (`iba a la tienda ayer` as completed trip)  
   **Why it happens:** mapping English past directly.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `fui a la tienda ayer`.

3. **Mistake:** `conocia` when meaning "met"  
   **Why it happens:** not recognizing meaning shift.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `conoci` for "met".

4. **Mistake:** `sabia` when meaning "found out"  
   **Why it happens:** mixing state vs discovery.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `supe`.

5. **Mistake:** `podia` when meaning "managed to do it"  
   **Why it happens:** choosing capability instead of completed success.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `pude`.

6. **Mistake:** `no queria` when meaning "refused"  
   **Why it happens:** literal translation of "didn't want."  
   **Correct Spanish:** `no quise` for refusal in a concrete moment.

7. **Mistake:** background in preterite (`estuvo frio y parecia...` in scene setup)  
   **Why it happens:** treating descriptions as events.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use imperfect for scene setting: `hacia frio`, `parecia...`

8. **Mistake:** forgetting sequence pattern `imperfect + cuando + preterite`  
   **Why it happens:** unclear event hierarchy.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `estaba leyendo cuando llamo`.

9. **Mistake:** overusing `tenia` when a specific moment of impression is meant  
   **Why it happens:** missing nuance in `tener` meaning shift.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `tuve la impresion...` for one moment.

10. **Mistake:** no time anchors in narratives  
    **Why it happens:** tense choice becomes ambiguous.  
    **Correct Spanish:** include `ayer`, `anoche`, `cuando`, `mientras`, etc.
