# Spanish Daily Lesson 65: Direct + Indirect Object Pronoun Stacking

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Keeping full nouns in every sentence or placing pronouns in English-like positions.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish sounds natural when you compress repeated objects into pronouns and follow a fixed stack: indirect first, direct second.

**target pattern / formula**  
- single object replacement: `lo/la/los/las`  
- recipient replacement: `me/te/le/nos/les`  
- double pronouns: `IOP + DOP`  
- `le/les + lo/la/los/las -> se + lo/la/los/las`  
- placement: before conjugated verb or attached to infinitive/gerund

**5 natural examples**  
1. Veo a Marta cada dia. La veo cada dia.  
2. Le di el informe a Juan. Se lo di.  
3. Te voy a mandar el archivo. Te lo voy a mandar.  
4. Estoy escribiendo la carta para ella. Se la estoy escribiendo.  
5. Puedes pasarme la sal? Puedes pasarmela?

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as directed.

1. Base: Le di el libro a Juan. -> replace objects with pronouns.  
2. Base: Te voy a mandar la foto. -> move pronouns to attached infinitive position.  
3. Base: Estoy escribiendo la carta para ella. -> rewrite with pronouns before the conjugated verb.  
4. Base: Les explicamos las reglas. -> change to singular recipient `ella` and keep pronoun stack.  
5. Base: Se lo dije. -> change direct object to feminine singular.  
6. Base: Me prestaron el coche. -> change recipient to `tu`.  
7. Base: Voy a mostrar el proyecto a mis jefes. -> replace with double pronouns.  
8. Base: Puedes pasarme la cuenta? -> change recipient to `nosotros`.  
9. Base: Se la enviaron. -> expand to full nouns: `la invitacion` to my grandparents.  
10. Base: Te las traje. -> change direct object to singular masculine.  
11. Base: Le estamos preparando la cena. -> move pronouns to attached gerund.  
12. Base: Se lo voy a contar. -> make it negative in yo form with no `voy a` frame.  
13. Base: Nos ofrecieron el contrato. -> change to recipient `ellos`.  
14. Base: Le deje un mensaje. -> change tense to future with `yo` and keep pronouns.  
15. Base: La estamos buscando. -> change direct object to plural feminine.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the most natural pronoun form.

1. I gave it to her. (`Le lo di.` vs `Se lo di.`)  
2. I am going to send it to you. (`Te lo voy a enviar.` vs `Voy a te lo enviar.`)  
3. We are writing it for them. (`Se la estamos escribiendo.` vs `Estamos se la escribiendo.`)  
4. Can you pass it to me? (`Puedes pasarmelo?` vs `Puedes me lo pasar?`)  
5. They told it to us. (`Nos lo dijeron.` vs `Lo nos dijeron.`)  
6. I explained it to him. (`Se lo explique.` vs `Le lo explique.`)  
7. She is showing it to her clients. (`Se lo esta mostrando.` vs `Esta mostrando se lo.`)  
8. We left it for you all. (`Se lo dejamos.` vs `Les lo dejamos.`)  
9. I brought them to you. (`Te las traje.` vs `Las te traje.`)  
10. I am reading it to her. (`Se lo estoy leyendo.` vs `Estoy le lo leyendo.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Le lo di`  
   **Why it happens:** Learner stacks pronouns in English-like order.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se lo di.`

2. **Mistake:** `Lo le dije`  
   **Why it happens:** Direct pronoun is placed before indirect pronoun.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se lo dije.`

3. **Mistake:** `Voy a te lo enviar`  
   **Why it happens:** Pronouns are inserted in a non-Spanish position.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Te lo voy a enviar / Voy a enviartelo.`

4. **Mistake:** `Estoy se la escribiendo`  
   **Why it happens:** Attached/preceding options are mixed incorrectly.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se la estoy escribiendo / Estoy escribiendosela.`

5. **Mistake:** `Las te traje`  
   **Why it happens:** Direct pronoun placed before indirect pronoun.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Te las traje.`

6. **Mistake:** `Puedes me lo pasar?`  
   **Why it happens:** Question structure copied from English order.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Puedes pasarmelo? / Me lo puedes pasar?`

7. **Mistake:** `Le explique lo`  
   **Why it happens:** Pronouns split around the verb.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se lo explique.`

8. **Mistake:** `Se la voy explicar`  
   **Why it happens:** Missing preposition `a` in infinitive frame.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Se la voy a explicar.`

9. **Mistake:** `No voy a contarselo` for direct negative command idea  
   **Why it happens:** Negation scope and verb frame are confused.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `No se lo cuento.`

10. **Mistake:** Using full nouns every time after first mention  
    **Why it happens:** Avoiding pronoun compression due to low confidence.  
    **Correct Spanish:** Reuse object pronouns: `Se lo di, Te la mande, Nos las mostraron.`
