# Spanish Daily Lesson 43: Poner and Ponerse in Real Use

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Treating `poner` as only "put" and translating each use word-for-word.

**Spanish logic**  
`Poner` is a high-frequency action framework: physical placement, commands, subjunctive requests, indirect-object food/drink additions, emotional impact, and pronominal `ponerse` for becoming or putting on clothes.

**target pattern / formula**  
- preterite completed placement: `puse, pusiste, puso, pusimos, pusieron`  
- command core: `pon / no pongas / ponga / no ponga`  
- trigger + subjunctive: `quieres que ponga...`  
- indirect object frame: `que le pusiste a...?` / `le puse...`  
- emotional effect: `me puso triste` / `me ponen nervioso`  
- pronominal: `ponerse + adjective/clothing`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Donde pusiste las llaves?  
2. Pon las maletas en el coche.  
3. Quieres que ponga la mesa?  
4. Que le pusiste a la sopa?  
5. Me puse nervioso antes de la reunion.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Puse las llaves en la mesa. -> change subject to `tu`.  
2. Base: Pusimos los platos en la mesa. -> change object to `las tazas`.  
3. Base: Pon la ropa en la cama. -> make it negative tu command.  
4. Base: Ponga las maletas en el coche. -> change to tu affirmative.  
5. Base: No pongas eso en el garaje. -> change to usted negative.  
6. Base: Quieres que ponga la mesa? -> change subject in subordinate clause to `ellos`.  
7. Base: Quieres que pongamos la ropa en la lavadora? -> change object to `los zapatos`.  
8. Base: Que le pusiste a la sopa? -> change noun to `al cafe`.  
9. Base: Le puse sal. -> change ingredient to `limon`.  
10. Base: La pelicula me puso feliz. -> change adjective to `triste`.  
11. Base: Las mentiras me ponen furioso. -> change subject to singular `la mentira`.  
12. Base: Me puse feliz al verte. -> change emotion to `nervioso`.  
13. Base: Ella se puso un vestido rojo. -> change clothing to `la chaqueta negra`.  
14. Base: Nos ponemos los abrigos en invierno. -> change season to `cuando hace frio`.  
15. Base: Te pusiste triste ayer. -> turn into question form.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. I put the keys on the table. (`Puse las llaves en la mesa.` vs `Ponia las llaves en la mesa.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `ponia` for a completed "put" event  
   **Why it happens:** imperfect used instead of completed preterite.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `puse / puso / pusieron` as needed.

2. **Mistake:** `no pon`  
   **Why it happens:** negative command built from affirmative stem.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `no pongas` (tu), `no ponga` (usted).

3. **Mistake:** `quieres que pongo`  
   **Why it happens:** not triggering subjunctive after `quieres que`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `quieres que ponga`.

4. **Mistake:** `que pusiste la sopa`  
   **Why it happens:** missing indirect-object structure with food/drink container.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `que le pusiste a la sopa`.

5. **Mistake:** `la puse sal`  
   **Why it happens:** wrong clitic for indirect object.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `le puse sal`.

6. **Mistake:** `me puso triste` when speaker means ongoing present trigger  
   **Why it happens:** tense mismatch.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `me pone triste` for habitual present.

7. **Mistake:** `puse nervioso`  
   **Why it happens:** omitting reflexive in "become" meaning.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `me puse nervioso`.

8. **Mistake:** `te pon la camisa`  
   **Why it happens:** wrong pronoun placement in affirmative reflexive command.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `ponte la camisa`.

9. **Mistake:** adjective mismatch in `poner + adjective`  
   **Why it happens:** adjective not agreeing with affected person.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `me puso triste / me puso triste` or `nos puso nerviosos` etc.

10. **Mistake:** mixing `poner` and `ponerse` randomly  
    **Why it happens:** not separating transitive put from pronominal become/put on.  
    **Correct Spanish:** `poner algo en...` vs `ponerse feliz / ponerse la chaqueta`.
