# Spanish Daily Lesson 6 (Writing-First)

## 1) cognitive_shift

- **English trap:** Translating “to” one-to-one and getting stuck when a sentence has two “to” ideas (go to X / to do Y).
- **Spanish logic:** Spanish motion uses a clear chain: `ir a + lugar`, and purpose uses a second `a + infinitivo`.
- **target pattern/formula:**
  - `Voy/Vas + a + lugar`
  - `Quiero/Puedo/Tengo que + ir a + lugar`
  - `... ir a + lugar + a + infinitivo` (purpose)

### 5 natural examples
1. Voy al supermercado.
2. Quiero ir a la biblioteca.
3. Tengo que ir al banco hoy.
4. ¿Puedes ir a la farmacia conmigo?
5. Voy a la tienda a comprar pan.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: `Voy a la tienda.` -> make it a question (`tú`).
2. Base: `Quiero ir al banco.` -> make it negative.
3. Base: `Tengo que ir al supermercado.` -> change `yo` to `nosotros`.
4. Base: `¿Puedes ir a la farmacia?` -> answer no in full sentence.
5. Base: `Voy a la tienda a comprar pan.` -> change product to `leche`.
6. Base: `Quiero ir al parque.` -> add purpose: `to run`.
7. Base: `Puedo ir al cine hoy.` -> move to tomorrow.
8. Base: `Tengo que ir a la oficina.` -> turn into question.
9. Base: `¿Quieres ir al gimnasio conmigo?` -> answer yes in full sentence.
10. Base: `Voy a la biblioteca.` -> change destination to plural people (`ellos`) and place `escuela`.
11. Base: `Tengo que ir a la tienda a comprar huevos.` -> make it negative.
12. Base: `¿Puedes ir al banco ahora?` -> change `ahora` to `después`.
13. Base: `Quiero ir al hospital.` -> change to past (`I went`).
14. Base: `Puedo ir a la playa el sábado.` -> change to inability.
15. Base: `Voy al supermercado.` -> expand with purpose `to buy vegetables`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the most natural pattern and write in Spanish.

1. “I’m going to the store.” (`voy` vs `quiero ir`)
2. “I want to go to the store.” (`voy` vs `quiero ir`)
3. “I can go to the store.” (`puedo ir` vs `voy`)
4. “I have to go to the store.” (`tengo que ir` vs `puedo ir`)
5. “Can you go to the bank?” (`¿puedes ir...?` vs `¿quieres ir...?`)
6. “Do you want to go to the beach?” (`¿quieres ir...?` vs `¿tienes que ir...?`)
7. “I’m going to the store to buy bread.” (`ir a + lugar + a + infinitivo` vs `para`)
8. “I have to go to the pharmacy to buy medicine.” (`tengo que ir a ... a comprar ...`)
9. “I can’t go to the office today.” (`no puedo ir` vs `no quiero ir`) meaning = ability problem.
10. “I don’t want to go to the office today.” (`no quiero ir` vs `no puedo ir`) meaning = desire problem.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Forgetting `a` after `ir`**  
   Why: English “go the store” interference in fast output.  
   Correct: `Voy a la tienda.`
2. **Using only one `a` in purpose sentences**  
   Why: Learner sees double “to” as redundant.  
   Correct: `Voy a la tienda a comprar pan.`
3. **Wrong contraction with masculine place nouns**  
   Why: Article/contraction not automated.  
   Correct: `Voy al banco.` (`a + el = al`)
4. **Missing article before place noun**  
   Why: Literal word mapping from English.  
   Correct: `Voy al supermercado.`
5. **Using `para` by default with motion + purpose**  
   Why: Overgeneralizing “to/for.”  
   Better here: `Voy a la farmacia a comprar medicina.`
6. **Mixing `voy` and `quiero ir` meanings**  
   Why: “I’m going” vs “I want to go” confusion.  
   Correct: `Voy...` (actual movement), `Quiero ir...` (desire)
7. **Using `puedo` when obligation is intended**  
   Why: Modal verbs feel interchangeable.  
   Correct: `Tengo que ir a la oficina.`
8. **Question word order copied from English**  
   Why: English inversion transfer.  
   Correct: `¿Puedes ir al banco?`
9. **Dropping `conmigo/contigo` chunks**  
   Why: Learner avoids pronoun clusters.  
   Correct: `¿Quieres ir conmigo a la playa?`
10. **Using infinitive instead of conjugated `ir` as main verb**  
    Why: Pattern overload with multiple infinitives.  
    Correct: `Voy a la tienda`, not `Ir a la tienda`.
