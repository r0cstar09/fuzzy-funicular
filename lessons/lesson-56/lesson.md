# Spanish Daily Lesson 56: Prepositions + Infinitive for Faster Speech

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Trying to conjugate every second verb and getting stuck mid-sentence.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish often uses prepositions plus infinitive to connect actions quickly and naturally.  
This reduces conjugation load and keeps speech fluent.  
If the subject changes in purpose clauses, switch from `para + infinitive` to `para que + subjunctive`.

**target pattern / formula**  
- sequence trigger: `al + infinitive`
- before/after: `antes de + infinitive`, `despues de + infinitive`
- substitution/lack: `en vez de + infinitive`, `sin + infinitive`
- purpose same subject: `para + infinitive`
- purpose different subject: `para que + subjuntivo`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Al llegar a casa, me acoste.  
2. Antes de salir, compre pan.  
3. Despues de comer, volvi al trabajo.  
4. Se fue sin despedirse.  
5. Apague la luz para que los ninos durmieran.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Al llegar a casa, me acoste. -> change place to `la oficina`.  
2. Base: Antes de salir, compre pan. -> change verb `comprar` to `llamar a mi madre`.  
3. Base: Despues de comer, volvi al trabajo. -> change first verb to `reunirme`.  
4. Base: En vez de trabajar, decidi descansar. -> change infinitive to `discutir`.  
5. Base: Se fue sin despedirse. -> change subject to plural `ellos`.  
6. Base: Salimos temprano para llegar a tiempo. -> change infinitive to `evitar trafico`.  
7. Base: Compre flores para decorar la casa. -> change object to `la oficina`.  
8. Base: Apague la luz para que los ninos durmieran. -> change subject in second clause to singular `el bebe`.  
9. Base: Antes de acostarme, vi una pelicula. -> change reflexive verb to `ducharme`.  
10. Base: Antes de acostarte, cepillate los dientes. -> change pronoun to plural `acostarse ustedes`.  
11. Base: Despues de ver la pelicula, me acoste. -> change to morning routine with `despertarme`.  
12. Base: Sin pensarlo mucho, acepte la oferta. -> change verb `acepte` to `rechace`.  
13. Base: En lugar de estudiar, fui a la playa. -> change destination to `el gimnasio`.  
14. Base: Revise el documento para compartirlo con el equipo. -> change to different subject using `para que`.  
15. Base: Cambie la fecha para que todos asistieran. -> change tense to present context.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** conjugating after preposition (`antes de salgo`)  
   **Why it happens:** learner assumes all verbs must be conjugated.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use infinitive after preposition.

2. **Mistake:** using `para + infinitive` when subjects are different  
   **Why it happens:** one purpose formula memorized for all cases.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use `para que + subjuntivo` with subject change.

3. **Mistake:** using `antes que` instead of `antes de` + infinitive  
   **Why it happens:** direct English mapping from before that.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `antes de + infinitive` in this structure.

4. **Mistake:** forgetting pronoun attachment in reflexives (`antes de me acostar`)  
   **Why it happens:** pronoun placement confusion.  
   **Correct Spanish:** attach pronoun to infinitive (`acostarme`).

5. **Mistake:** using finite verb after `sin`  
   **Why it happens:** sentence translated word by word.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `sin + infinitive` for without doing.

6. **Mistake:** using `al` with conjugated verb  
   **Why it happens:** `al` interpreted as regular article+preposition.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `al + infinitive` for upon doing.

7. **Mistake:** mixing `en vez de` with clause connectors incorrectly  
   **Why it happens:** blend of two structures.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `en vez de + infinitive` for instead of doing.

8. **Mistake:** dropping `de` in `despues de`  
   **Why it happens:** chunk remembered incompletely.  
   **Correct Spanish:** keep `despues de + infinitive`.

9. **Mistake:** wrong tense in `para que` subordinate clause  
   **Why it happens:** tense agreement not considered.  
   **Correct Spanish:** match context, often subjunctive present or imperfect.

10. **Mistake:** avoiding these chunks in speech  
    **Why it happens:** fear of making conjugation mistakes.  
    **Correct Spanish:** these chunks are exactly for smoother speech.
