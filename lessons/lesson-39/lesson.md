# Spanish Daily Lesson 39: Preterite for Completed Actions

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Using one generic past form for everything without signaling whether the action was completed.

**Spanish logic**  
Use preterite when the action is finished and treated as a complete event (`ayer`, `anoche`, `el ano pasado`). Use this tense to answer: "What happened?"

**target pattern / formula**  
- completed action + specific time marker -> preterite  
- regular `-ar`: `e, aste, o, amos, aron`  
- regular `-er/-ir`: `i, iste, io, imos, ieron`  
- high-frequency irregulars: `fui, hice, tuve, estuve, pude, dije, vine, vi, di`  
- spelling/stem shifts: `busque`, `pague`, `comence`, `durmio/durmieron`, `pidio/pidieron`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Comi tacos anoche.  
2. Fui a la tienda ayer.  
3. Le dije la verdad.  
4. Estuvimos en casa toda la noche.  
5. Ellos durmieron poco.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Hable con ella ayer. -> change subject to tu.  
2. Base: Comi tacos anoche. -> change object to `ensalada`.  
3. Base: Fuimos a la tienda. -> change subject to yo.  
4. Base: El dijo la verdad. -> make it negative.  
5. Base: Estuviste en casa anoche. -> change to question form.  
6. Base: Tuviste tiempo para estudiar. -> change subject to nosotros.  
7. Base: Pude terminar el trabajo. -> change subject to ella.  
8. Base: Vinieron a la fiesta. -> change to singular `el`.  
9. Base: Vi una pelicula. -> change subject to nosotros.  
10. Base: Di las llaves a mi amigo. -> change recipient to `mi hermana`.  
11. Base: Busque mi telefono. -> change verb to `pagar` and object to `la cuenta`.  
12. Base: Comence la clase temprano. -> change subject to nosotros.  
13. Base: El durmio ocho horas. -> change subject to ellos.  
14. Base: Ella pidio ayuda. -> change subject to ellos.  
15. Base: Hiciste la tarea ayer. -> change to question form with `tu`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form for a completed event.

1. I ate tacos. (`Comi tacos.` vs `Comia tacos.`)  
2. I went to the store yesterday. (`Fui a la tienda ayer.` vs `Iba a la tienda ayer.`)  
3. She told me the truth. (`Me dijo la verdad.` vs `Me decia la verdad.`)  
4. We were at home all night (as one completed event). (`Estuvimos en casa toda la noche.` vs `Estabamos en casa toda la noche.`)  
5. I saw a movie last night. (`Vi una pelicula anoche.` vs `Veia una pelicula anoche.`)  
6. They came to the party. (`Vinieron a la fiesta.` vs `Venian a la fiesta.`)  
7. I gave him the money. (`Le di el dinero.` vs `Le daba el dinero.`)  
8. Were you able to finish it? (`Pudiste terminarlo?` vs `Podias terminarlo?`)  
9. I had time to study. (`Tuve tiempo para estudiar.` vs `Tenia tiempo para estudiar.`)  
10. What did you do yesterday? (`Que hiciste ayer?` vs `Que hacias ayer?`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Comia tacos anoche` for one finished meal  
   **Why it happens:** Using imperfect for a completed event.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Comi tacos anoche.`

2. **Mistake:** `Yo fue`  
   **Why it happens:** Mixing person endings with `ir/ser` preterite.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Yo fui`.

3. **Mistake:** `Deci la verdad`  
   **Why it happens:** Regularizing irregular `decir`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Dije la verdad.`

4. **Mistake:** `Podi terminar` for "was able to finish"  
   **Why it happens:** Forgetting preterite of `poder`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Pude terminar.`

5. **Mistake:** `Busce` / `pague` without spelling awareness  
   **Why it happens:** Yo-form spelling shifts not memorized.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `busque`, `pague`, `comence`.

6. **Mistake:** `Dormieron` for `el`  
   **Why it happens:** Applying plural third person to singular subject.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `el durmio`.

7. **Mistake:** `Nosotros dijo`  
   **Why it happens:** Using third-person irregular with nosotros.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Nosotros dijimos`.

8. **Mistake:** `Le dio las llaves a mi`  
   **Why it happens:** Incomplete object phrase after preposition.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Le dio las llaves a mi amiga` (or another full noun phrase).

9. **Mistake:** `Hablastes`  
   **Why it happens:** Adding extra `s` to preterite `tu` form.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Hablaste`.

10. **Mistake:** Avoiding time markers in past drills  
    **Why it happens:** Not anchoring completed-action meaning.  
    **Correct Spanish:** Add cues like `ayer`, `anoche`, `el ano pasado`.
