# Spanish Daily Lesson 32: Present Progressive Without Overusing It

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Using progressive forms for almost everything happening now and forcing them onto motion verbs.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish present progressive is clear and useful (`estar + gerundio`) but used more selectively than English.  
Use it for actions in progress; use simple present often for immediate context, especially with `ir` and `venir`.

**target pattern / formula**  
- `estar (conjugated) + gerundio`  
- `-ar -> -ando`, `-er/-ir -> -iendo`  
- common irregular gerunds: `diciendo`, `pidiendo`, `durmiendo`, `leyendo`, `oyendo`, `siguiendo`  
- in-progress effort: `estar tratando de + infinitive`  
- avoid default progressive with motion: prefer `voy`, `viene`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Estoy hablando con mi jefe.  
2. Ella esta leyendo un informe.  
3. Estamos oyendo un ruido raro.  
4. Ellos estan durmiendo ahora.  
5. Estoy tratando de aprender espanol.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Estoy hablando con mi amigo. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
2. Base: Estamos comiendo tacos. -> change object to `ensalada`.  
3. Base: Ella esta viendo la pelicula. -> change to `ellos`.  
4. Base: Ellos estan leyendo un libro. -> change noun to plural `libros`.  
5. Base: El esta oyendo un ruido. -> change verb to `escuchar` keeping progressive.  
6. Base: Estoy estudiando espanol ahora mismo. -> make it negative.  
7. Base: Estas escribiendo una carta. -> make it question form with `usted`.  
8. Base: El esta bebiendo cafe. -> change object to `te`.  
9. Base: Estamos limpiando la casa. -> change subject to `ella`.  
10. Base: Ellos estan durmiendo en la sala. -> change location to `el dormitorio`.  
11. Base: Estoy diciendo algo importante. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
12. Base: El esta pidiendo ayuda. -> change to plural subject `ellos`.  
13. Base: Ella esta explicando el problema. -> change object to `la solucion`.  
14. Base: Ellos estan construyendo una casa. -> change to singular subject `el`.  
15. Base: Ellos estan siguiendo las instrucciones. -> change to singular `yo`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. I am reading now. (`Estoy leyendo ahora.` vs `Leo ahora.` both possible, progressive targets active in-progress frame)  
2. What are you doing? (`Que haces?` vs `Que estas haciendo?` in this lesson both exist; simple present is often preferred conversationally)  
3. He is coming now. (`Viene ahora.` vs `Esta viniendo ahora.` default preferred pattern)  
4. I am going to the store now. (`Voy a la tienda ahora.` vs `Estoy yendo a la tienda ahora.` default preferred pattern)  
5. They are sleeping in the car. (`Estan durmiendo en el coche.` vs `Duermen en el coche.` when explicitly in progress choose progressive)  
6. We are hearing a noise. (`Estamos oyendo un ruido.` vs `Estamos oiendo un ruido.`)  
7. She is asking for help. (`Ella esta pidiendo ayuda.` vs `Ella esta pediendo ayuda.`)  
8. I am saying the truth. (`Estoy diciendo la verdad.` vs `Estoy deciendo la verdad.`)  
9. They are following the instructions. (`Estan siguiendo las instrucciones.` vs `Estan seguiendo las instrucciones.`)  
10. We are trying to learn Spanish. (`Estamos tratando de aprender espanol.` vs `Estamos tratando aprender espanol.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `esta oiendo`  
   **Why it happens:** Missing spelling change in vowel sequence verbs.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `esta oyendo`

2. **Mistake:** `esta pediendo`  
   **Why it happens:** Regularizing irregular stem-changing gerund.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `esta pidiendo`

3. **Mistake:** `esta deciendo`  
   **Why it happens:** Applying regular pattern to irregular gerund.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `esta diciendo`

4. **Mistake:** `esta seguiendo`  
   **Why it happens:** Incorrect stem handling in `seguir`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `esta siguiendo`

5. **Mistake:** forcing progressive with `ir/venir` by default  
   **Why it happens:** direct translation from English continuous.  
   **Correct Spanish:** usually prefer `voy`, `viene`.

6. **Mistake:** overusing progressive for habits  
   **Why it happens:** confusion between in-progress and routine action.  
   **Correct Spanish:** habits typically use simple present.

7. **Mistake:** missing `de` in `tratando de + infinitive`  
   **Why it happens:** incomplete chunk memorization.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `estoy tratando de aprender`.

8. **Mistake:** conjugating both verbs (`estoy aprendo`)  
   **Why it happens:** double-conjugation error.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `estoy aprendiendo`.

9. **Mistake:** dropping accent in `esta` confusion  
   **Why it happens:** orthography neglect.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `esta` verb form with correct accent in standard writing.

10. **Mistake:** using progressive when context already carries immediacy  
    **Why it happens:** pattern overgeneralization.  
    **Correct Spanish:** choose present simple when it sounds more natural conversationally.
