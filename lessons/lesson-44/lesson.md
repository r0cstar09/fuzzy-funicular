# Spanish Daily Lesson 44: Conditional for Would, Could, and Politeness

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Using present or future forms for every "would/could" meaning and missing polite conditional structures.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish conditional is a practical pattern for hypothetical actions, polite requests, and future-in-the-past reporting. Keep infinitive stems and add conditional endings; irregular verbs use irregular stems but same endings.

**target pattern / formula**  
- regular conditional: `infinitive + ia, ias, ia, iamos, ian`  
- irregular stems: `tendr-, podr-, dir-, har-, querr-, pondr-, saldr-, vendr-, sabr-`  
- polite requests: `podria/podrias ...?`, `me gustaria ...`  
- future in the past: `dijo que llegaria`  
- hypotheticals: `si + imperfect subjunctive`, conditional in result clause

**5 natural examples**  
1. Hablariamos mas si tuvieramos tiempo.  
2. Podrias ayudarme, por favor?  
3. Me gustaria reservar una mesa.  
4. Juan dijo que llegaria a las cinco.  
5. Yo me mudaria a Mexico si fuera rico.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Yo hablaria contigo manana. -> change subject to nosotros.  
2. Base: Tu comerias mas verduras. -> change object to `fruta`.  
3. Base: El viviria en Mexico. -> change subject to ella.  
4. Base: Nosotros estudiariamos mas si tuvieramos tiempo. -> change condition to `si no trabajaramos tanto`.  
5. Base: Ellos trabajarian aqui. -> make it negative.  
6. Base: Usted aprenderia espanol rapidamente. -> turn into a polite question.  
7. Base: Yo escribiria una carta. -> change object to plural `dos correos`.  
8. Base: Tu abririas la ventana. -> turn into polite request question.  
9. Base: Yo tendria mas tiempo si pudiera. -> change subject to nosotros.  
10. Base: El diria la verdad. -> change subject to ellos.  
11. Base: Nosotros saldriamos temprano. -> change adverb to `mas tarde`.  
12. Base: Ella lo haria manana. -> change time to `hoy`.  
13. Base: Yo querria ir contigo. -> change to second person `tu`.  
14. Base: Juan dijo que llegaria a las cinco. -> change speaker to Maria and time to `a las seis`.  
15. Base: Yo me mudaria a Mexico si fuera rico. -> change country to `Espana`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the correct Spanish form.

1. Could you help me? (`Podrias ayudarme?` vs `Puedes ayudarme?` for conditional target)  
2. I would like to speak with you. (`Me gustaria hablar contigo.` vs `Me gusta hablar contigo.`)  
3. He said he would come early. (`Dijo que vendria temprano.` vs `Dijo que vendra temprano.`)  
4. We knew it would be expensive. (`Sabiamos que seria caro.` vs `Sabiamos que es caro.`)  
5. They would come if they had time. (`Vendrian si tuvieran tiempo.` vs `Vendran si tienen tiempo.` hypothetical target)  
6. I would have more time if I could. (`Tendria mas tiempo si pudiera.` vs `Tendre mas tiempo si puedo.`)  
7. She would do it tomorrow. (`Lo haria manana.` vs `Lo hizo manana.`)  
8. Would it be possible to change the appointment? (`Seria posible cambiar la cita?` vs `Es posible cambiar la cita?` for conditional politeness)  
9. We would leave early. (`Saldriamos temprano.` vs `Salimos temprano.`)  
10. You said you would arrive at five. (`Dijiste que llegarias a las cinco.` vs `Dijiste que llegaste a las cinco.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** using future instead of conditional in reported speech  
   **Why it happens:** direct English transfer from "will."  
   **Correct Spanish:** `dijo que llegaria`, not `dijo que llegara`.

2. **Mistake:** `podia ayudarme?` for polite request  
   **Why it happens:** confusing imperfect ability with polite conditional.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `podria/podrias ayudarme?`

3. **Mistake:** `me gustaria que hablar contigo`  
   **Why it happens:** adding unnecessary `que` before infinitive.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `me gustaria hablar contigo`.

4. **Mistake:** regularizing irregular stems (`teneria`, `haceria`)  
   **Why it happens:** applying regular infinitive pattern to irregular verbs.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `tendria`, `haria`.

5. **Mistake:** using present in hypothetical result clause  
   **Why it happens:** English pattern interference.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `si tuviera tiempo, iria`.

6. **Mistake:** missing accent marks in conditional forms  
   **Why it happens:** spelling oversight.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `hablaria`, `podria`, `seria` (with written accents in standard orthography).

7. **Mistake:** `si podria` in if-clause  
   **Why it happens:** both clauses put in conditional.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `si pudiera..., podria...`

8. **Mistake:** `dijo que lo haria manana` changed to preterite by default  
   **Why it happens:** overusing completed-past narrative form.  
   **Correct Spanish:** keep conditional for future-in-the-past.

9. **Mistake:** overusing `queria` when intent is hypothetical wish  
   **Why it happens:** confusing imperfect desire with conditional politeness.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `querria` for "would want."

10. **Mistake:** literal translation of "would like" as `gustaria` alone  
    **Why it happens:** missing indirect object pronoun.  
    **Correct Spanish:** `me gustaria...`
