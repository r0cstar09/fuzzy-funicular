# Spanish Daily Lesson 60: Preposition-Front Questions

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Ending Spanish questions with a preposition the way English does (for example, "What are you thinking about?").

**Spanish logic**  
In Spanish, if the verb needs a preposition, that preposition goes before `que` at the start of the question.  
You build: `preposition + que + verb`.

**target pattern / formula**  
- required-preposition verbs:
  - `pensar en`
  - `quejarse de`
  - `hablar de`
  - `arrepentirse de`
  - `disfrazarse de`
- question frame:
  - `En que + verb?`
  - `De que + verb?`
- core transformation:
  - statement: `Pienso en...`
  - question: `En que piensas?`

**5 natural examples**  
1. En que piensas?  
2. De que se queja Maria?  
3. De que quieres hablar?  
4. De que te arrepientes?  
5. De que te vas a disfrazar?

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** placing preposition at sentence end  
   **Why it happens:** direct transfer from English question structure.  
   **Correct Spanish:** move required preposition to the front.

2. **Mistake:** dropping the required preposition entirely  
   **Why it happens:** forgetting verb-preposition chunk.  
   **Correct Spanish:** learn verb with its preposition.

3. **Mistake:** using wrong preposition with known verb  
   **Why it happens:** approximate translation habits.  
   **Correct Spanish:** memorize high-frequency collocations.

4. **Mistake:** building `que + verb + preposition`  
   **Why it happens:** English-like linear construction.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use `preposition + que + verb`.

5. **Mistake:** forgetting reflexive pronoun in `quejarse/arrepentirse/disfrazarse`  
   **Why it happens:** chunk incomplete in memory.  
   **Correct Spanish:** keep reflexive pronouns in question.

6. **Mistake:** wrong conjugation with reflexive verbs  
   **Why it happens:** focus on preposition only.  
   **Correct Spanish:** conjugate the verb accurately too.

7. **Mistake:** overusing literal "about" translations  
   **Why it happens:** English anchor strategy.  
   **Correct Spanish:** select preposition by Spanish verb pattern.

8. **Mistake:** translating "what do you regret?" without `de`  
   **Why it happens:** English has no explicit preposition.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `De que te arrepientes?`

9. **Mistake:** avoiding these questions due to uncertainty  
   **Why it happens:** fear of making structural errors.  
   **Correct Spanish:** use one reusable frame across many verbs.

10. **Mistake:** memorizing vocabulary without sentence templates  
    **Why it happens:** isolated study method.  
    **Correct Spanish:** train with full question patterns.
