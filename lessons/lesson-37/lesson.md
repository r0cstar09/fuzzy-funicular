# Spanish Daily Lesson 37: Future Without Overthinking

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Using only "going to" translations and avoiding Spanish future forms.

**Spanish logic**  
Spanish uses three natural future frames: `ir a + infinitive`, present + future time, and simple future (`hablare`).  
Simple future also expresses probability/guessing (`Estara en casa` = probably at home).

**target pattern / formula**  
- `ir (conjugated) + a + infinitive`  
- present tense + future time expression (`manana`, `el lunes`)  
- simple future: `infinitive + endings (e, as, a, emos, an)`  
- irregular future stems: `tendr-`, `dir-`, `har-`, `saldr-`, `vendr-`, `habr-`  
- supposition: `sera...?`, `estara...`, `tendra...`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Voy a estudiar manana.  
2. Trabajo el sabado.  
3. Hablare con Maria.  
4. Tendremos reunion a las nueve.  
5. Estara ocupado ahora.

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: Hablare con Maria. -> change subject to nosotros.  
2. Base: Voy a estudiar manana. -> change to simple future.  
3. Base: Trabajo el sabado. -> change day to `el lunes`.  
4. Base: Ellos llegaran tarde. -> make it a yes/no question.  
5. Base: El saldra temprano. -> make it negative.  
6. Base: Tendremos reunion a las nueve. -> change to singular yo form.  
7. Base: La empresa abrira una oficina en junio. -> change subject to plural empresas.  
8. Base: Veremos los resultados el viernes. -> change object to singular `el resultado`.  
9. Base: Voy a enviar el informe ahora. -> change subject to ella.  
10. Base: Estara en casa. -> change to question form.  
11. Base: Tendran unos cuarenta anos. -> change estimate to thirty.  
12. Base: Dire la verdad manana. -> change subject to tu.  
13. Base: Vendremos el domingo. -> change to `ellos`.  
14. Base: Hara calor manana. -> change to question form.  
15. Base: Acabamos la presentacion el martes. -> rewrite with simple future.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the most natural Spanish option for the intended meaning.

1. I will call you tonight. (`Te llamare esta noche.` vs `Te voy a llamare esta noche.`)  
2. We are going to travel tomorrow. (`Vamos a viajar manana.` vs `Viajaremos a manana.`)  
3. I work tomorrow. (`Trabajo manana.` vs `Trabajare manana.` both possible; choose present+time as target)  
4. Will you bring me another napkin, please? (`Me traes otra servilleta, por favor?` vs `Me traerás otra servilleta, por favor?`)  
5. He is probably at home. (`Estara en casa.` vs `Esta en casa probablemente.` for supposition target)  
6. Who might that be? (`Quien sera?` vs `Quien esra?`)  
7. They will have more time. (`Tendran mas tiempo.` vs `Teneran mas tiempo.`)  
8. She will say it tomorrow. (`Lo dira manana.` vs `Lo decira manana.`)  
9. We will leave early. (`Saldremos temprano.` vs `Saliremos temprano.`)  
10. I am going to study tonight. (`Voy a estudiar esta noche.` vs `Voy estudiar esta noche.`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Voy a llamare.`  
   **Why it happens:** Mixing two future patterns in one verb slot.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Voy a llamar` or `llamare`.

2. **Mistake:** `Teneran` / `decira` / `salira` (wrong stems)  
   **Why it happens:** Treating irregulars as regular infinitive stems.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `tendran`, `dira`, `saldra`.

3. **Mistake:** `Vamos viajar`  
   **Why it happens:** Omitting `a` in `ir a + infinitive`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Vamos a viajar`.

4. **Mistake:** `Trabajo en lunes`  
   **Why it happens:** Translating "on Monday" literally with `en`.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Trabajo el lunes`.

5. **Mistake:** `Hoy es el domingo` (when saying the day)  
   **Why it happens:** Overusing article with day names.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Hoy es domingo`.

6. **Mistake:** `Me traeras otra servilleta, por favor?` for polite request  
   **Why it happens:** Copying English "will you...?" literally.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Me traes otra servilleta, por favor?`

7. **Mistake:** `Quien sera el?` every time for certainty  
   **Why it happens:** Misusing future-of-probability for known facts.  
   **Correct Spanish:** Use supposition only when guessing.

8. **Mistake:** `Hablare con Maria manana?` without question marks  
   **Why it happens:** Punctuation transfer from English typing habits.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Hablare con Maria manana?`

9. **Mistake:** `Nosotros hablare`  
   **Why it happens:** Applying yo ending to nosotros.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Nosotros hablaremos`.

10. **Mistake:** Avoiding present+time future (`Trabajo manana`)  
    **Why it happens:** Belief that only formal future is correct.  
    **Correct Spanish:** Present + future time is very common and natural.
