# Spanish Daily Lesson 8 (Writing-First)

## 1) cognitive_shift

- **English trap:** Translating future ideas with direct present tense only, or mixing “going to” with “go to a place.”
- **Spanish logic:** For near-future intentions and planned actions, use `ir a + infinitivo` as one action frame.
- **target pattern/formula:**
  - `Voy/Vas/Va/Vamos + a + infinitivo`
  - `¿Qué vas a ...? / ¿Cuándo vas a ...? / ¿Dónde vas a ...?`
  - Contrast with `quiero + infinitivo`, `puedo + infinitivo`, `tengo que + infinitivo`

### 5 natural examples
1. Voy a descansar después del trabajo.
2. ¿Vas a estudiar esta noche?
3. Vamos a comprar comida ahora.
4. ¿Qué vas a hacer mañana?
5. Voy a confirmar la información hoy.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: `Voy a estudiar.` -> make it negative.
2. Base: `¿Vas a trabajar hoy?` -> answer yes in full sentence.
3. Base: `Voy a comprar pan.` -> change object to `leche`.
4. Base: `Vamos a descansar.` -> change to singular `yo`.
5. Base: `¿Qué vas a hacer?` -> change to `cuándo`.
6. Base: `Voy a viajar el viernes.` -> change day to `lunes`.
7. Base: `¿Vas a beber agua?` -> rewrite using `tomar`.
8. Base: `Voy a escribir un correo.` -> turn into question.
9. Base: `Voy a correr mañana.` -> change to `nosotros`.
10. Base: `¿Dónde vas a trabajar?` -> answer with `la oficina`.
11. Base: `Voy a practicar español.` -> add frequency `todos los días`.
12. Base: `No voy a comprar nada.` -> make it affirmative with `algo`.
13. Base: `Voy a leer un libro.` -> change tense to desire (`querer`).
14. Base: `Voy a descansar ahora.` -> change to obligation (`tener que`).
15. Base: `Vas a confirmar la información.` -> make it a question with `qué`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the best pattern in Spanish.

1. “I’m going to study tonight.” (`voy a estudiar` vs `estudio` for planned near future)
2. “I want to study tonight.” (`quiero estudiar` vs `voy a estudiar`)
3. “I can study tonight.” (`puedo estudiar` vs `voy a estudiar`)
4. “I have to study tonight.” (`tengo que estudiar` vs `voy a estudiar`)
5. “What are you going to do?” (`¿qué vas a hacer?` vs `¿qué quieres hacer?`) intention now.
6. “Are you going to buy water?” (`¿vas a comprar...?` vs `¿fuiste a comprar...?`)
7. “I’m going to confirm the reservation now.” (`voy a confirmar` vs `confirmo` for immediate plan)
8. “When are you going to travel?” (`¿cuándo vas a viajar?` vs `¿cuándo viajaste?`)
9. “We are going to rest later.” (`vamos a descansar` vs `descansamos` for future plan)
10. “I’m not going to work this weekend.” (`no voy a trabajar` vs `no trabajo`) future intent.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Using only present for all future plans**  
   Why: English present can signal future too.  
   Correct: `Voy a estudiar esta noche.`
2. **Forgetting `a` in the frame**  
   Why: Learner focuses only on main verb.  
   Correct: `Voy a comprar pan.`
3. **Confusing `voy a` (future) with `voy a la` (destination)**  
   Why: Same surface words, different function.  
   Correct: `Voy a descansar` vs `Voy a la tienda`
4. **Adding conjugated verb after `voy a`**  
   Why: English transfer from finite clauses.  
   Correct: `Voy a estudiar`, not `voy a estudio`.
5. **Mixing `vas` with `yo` meaning**  
   Why: Subject-verb mismatch under speed.  
   Correct: `Yo voy` / `Tú vas`
6. **Question word without proper verb frame**  
   Why: Direct literal ordering from English.  
   Correct: `¿Qué vas a hacer?`
7. **Using past marker with future frame**  
   Why: Timeline collision (`ayer` with `voy a`).  
   Correct: `Voy a trabajar mañana.`
8. **Using `ir a + infinitive` when obligation is intended**  
   Why: All modal-like frames feel equivalent.  
   Correct: `Tengo que estudiar.` (obligation)
9. **Using `ir a + infinitive` when desire is intended**  
   Why: Future intention vs desire not separated.  
   Correct: `Quiero estudiar.` (desire)
10. **Dropping opening question mark**  
    Why: English punctuation habit.  
    Correct: `¿Vas a estudiar hoy?`
