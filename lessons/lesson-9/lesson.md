# Spanish Daily Lesson 9 (Writing-First)

## 1) cognitive_shift

- **English trap:** Using one “is/are” structure for everything.
- **Spanish logic:** Spanish separates **existence** (`hay`) from **location of known things** (`está/están`).
- **target pattern/formula:**
  - `Hay + noun` (there is / there are)
  - `El/La + noun + está ...` (specific singular thing is located)
  - `Los/Las + noun + están ...` (specific plural things are located)

### 5 natural examples
1. Hay una mesa en la cocina.
2. La mesa está en la cocina.
3. Hay llaves en el pasillo.
4. Las llaves están en el pasillo.
5. ¿Hay una silla cerca de la ventana?

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: `Hay una lámpara en la sala.` -> change to plural.
2. Base: `La lámpara está sobre la mesa.` -> make it a question.
3. Base: `Hay zapatos en el pasillo.` -> make it negative.
4. Base: `Los zapatos están en el pasillo.` -> change location to `debajo de la cama`.
5. Base: `Hay una mesa en la cocina.` -> ask “where is the table?” in a second sentence.
6. Base: `La mesa está en la cocina.` -> change subject to plural `las mesas`.
7. Base: `Hay un gato dentro de la caja.` -> replace `dentro de` with `fuera de`.
8. Base: `El gato está dentro de la caja.` -> make it negative.
9. Base: `Hay un banco enfrente de la iglesia.` -> change `banco` to plural.
10. Base: `El banco está entre el supermercado y la farmacia.` -> change to `los bancos`.
11. Base: `Hay un teléfono en el escritorio.` -> change object to `dos teléfonos`.
12. Base: `El teléfono está sobre el escritorio.` -> rewrite with `en` (natural variant).
13. Base: `Hay una silla al lado de la ventana.` -> turn into question.
14. Base: `La silla está al lado de la ventana.` -> move it to `a la izquierda de la puerta`.
15. Base: `Hay una computadora en la oficina.` -> follow with a location sentence for that same computer.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the most natural pattern and write in Spanish.

1. “There is a table in the kitchen.” (`hay` vs `está`)
2. “The table is in the kitchen.” (`hay` vs `está`)
3. “There are books on the desk.” (`hay` vs `están`)
4. “The books are on the desk.” (`hay` vs `están`)
5. “Is there a pharmacy near here?” (`¿hay...?` vs `¿está... una farmacia...?`)
6. “Where is the pharmacy?” (`¿dónde hay...?` vs `¿dónde está... ?`) specific known place.
7. “There is no bank on this street.” (`no hay` vs `no está`) generic existence.
8. “The bank is across from the church.” (`está` vs `hay`) known location.
9. “There are no keys in the bag.” (`no hay` vs `no están`) not known if they exist there.
10. “The keys are not in the bag.” (`no están` vs `no hay`) known keys.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Using `está` for existence**  
   Why: Translating “there is” as “is.”  
   Correct: `Hay una mesa en la cocina.`
2. **Using `hay` for known object location**  
   Why: One-size-fits-all “there is” strategy.  
   Correct: `La mesa está en la cocina.`
3. **Using singular `está` with plural subject**  
   Why: Subject agreement not tracked.  
   Correct: `Los libros están en el escritorio.`
4. **Using `hay` with definite article for specific referent**  
   Why: Overgeneralizing `hay + noun`.  
   Better: `El libro está en la mesa.` (if specific)
5. **Missing contractions in location phrases**  
   Why: `de + el` / `a + el` not automated.  
   Correct: `del pasillo`, `al lado del sofá`
6. **Wrong preposition transfer from English**  
   Why: Direct lexical mapping.  
   Correct: `enfrente de`, `debajo de`, `al lado de`
7. **Dropping article with room nouns**  
   Why: English often omits article in location phrases.  
   Correct: `en la cocina`, `en el baño`
8. **Confusing `dentro de` and `detrás de`**  
   Why: Similar sounding chunks.  
   Correct: `dentro de la caja` vs `detrás de la caja`
9. **Using `hay no` instead of `no hay`**  
   Why: Negation placement from English order.  
   Correct: `No hay sillas aquí.`
10. **Using `estar` infinitive without conjugation**  
    Why: Learner writes dictionary form in full sentence.  
    Correct: `La silla está ...`, not `la silla estar ...`
