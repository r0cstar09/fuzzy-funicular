# Spanish Daily Lesson 67: Preterite vs Imperfect with Meaning Shifts

## 1) cognitive_shift

**English trap**  
Choosing tense by translation only and missing how tense changes verb meaning.

**Spanish logic**  
In narration, imperfect builds the scene and preterite advances events; some verbs switch meaning depending on tense.

**target pattern / formula**  
- imperfect for background, repeated habits, states  
- preterite for completed event and timeline movement  
- interruption frame: imperfect action + preterite event  
- meaning shifts: `conocer`, `saber`, `querer`, `poder`, `tener`  
- continuous nuance: `estaba + gerundio` vs `estuve + gerundio`

**5 natural examples**  
1. Cuando era nino, jugaba en ese parque.  
2. Ayer conoci a la nueva gerente.  
3. No sabia la respuesta hasta hoy.  
4. Queria salir, pero no pude.  
5. Estaba trabajando cuando sono el telefono.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as directed.

1. Base: Cuando era nino, iba a la playa. -> change subject to `nosotros`.  
2. Base: Ayer lei un articulo. -> change object to `una novela`.  
3. Base: Cocinabas cuando sono el telefono. -> change subject to `ella`.  
4. Base: Estabamos en el parque cuando empezo a llover. -> change location to `la oficina`.  
5. Base: Juan estaba en el ejercito cuando conocio a Maria. -> change person to `yo` and object to `Carlos`.  
6. Base: Ella queria ir, pero no pudo. -> make plural `ellos`.  
7. Base: Mi papa fumaba todos los dias. -> make it negative.  
8. Base: Visitamos a nuestros abuelos el domingo pasado. -> change day to `sabado`.  
9. Base: Sabias que Juan mintio? -> change to `ustedes`.  
10. Base: No sabia que querias ir. -> change to `nosotros` and plural meaning `ustedes`.  
11. Base: Conoci a Ana en 2018. -> change year to `2022`.  
12. Base: Maria dijo que conocia a Ana. -> change to `yo dije` and object to `Pedro`.  
13. Base: Quise gritar. -> change to refusal meaning with `no + querer`.  
14. Base: Pude levantar la caja. -> change to imperfect capability meaning with no completed action.  
15. Base: Habia mucho trafico cuando sali de casa. -> change subject to `nosotros` and verb to `llegar al trabajo`.

## 7) common_errors

1. **Mistake:** `Conocia a Juan en 2010`  
   **Why it happens:** Meeting event is confused with ongoing knowing.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Conoci a Juan en 2010.`

2. **Mistake:** `Supe que querias venir` for ongoing not knowing context  
   **Why it happens:** `saber` preterite and imperfect meanings are mixed.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `No sabia que querias venir.`

3. **Mistake:** `No queria firmar` used when refusal is completed  
   **Why it happens:** `querer` imperfect is used for a bounded refusal.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `No quise firmar.`

4. **Mistake:** `Podia abrir la puerta yo mismo` (after successful attempt)  
   **Why it happens:** Ability and successful completion are not distinguished.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Pude abrir la puerta yo mismo.`

5. **Mistake:** `Tenia la impresion` in a sudden realization moment  
   **Why it happens:** Ongoing state is used instead of received impression.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Tuve la impresion de que estaba cansado.`

6. **Mistake:** `Cuando llegue, bailaron`  
   **Why it happens:** Background action is switched to event tense.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Cuando llegue, bailaban.`

7. **Mistake:** `Eran las ocho y llovio`  
   **Why it happens:** Scene description gets forced into preterite.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Eran las ocho y llovia.`

8. **Mistake:** `Estuve leyendo cuando me llamo`  
   **Why it happens:** Completed-duration continuous is used instead of interrupted-progress frame.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Estaba leyendo cuando me llamo.`

9. **Mistake:** `Hubo mucho trafico cuando salia de casa`  
   **Why it happens:** Continuous context and punctual event are mismatched.  
   **Correct Spanish:** `Habia mucho trafico cuando sali de casa.`

10. **Mistake:** `Conocio a Ana muy bien`  
    **Why it happens:** Preterite `conocer` is overextended to ongoing knowledge.  
    **Correct Spanish:** `Conocia a Ana muy bien.`
