# Spanish Daily Lesson 7 (Writing-First)

## 1) cognitive_shift

- **English trap:** Using present-style movement sentences for past events, or translating “ago/already” loosely.
- **Spanish logic:** For completed movement in the past, use `ir` in preterite (`fui/fuiste`) and anchor it with `hace ...` or `ya`.
- **target pattern/formula:**
  - `Fui/Fuiste + a + lugar`
  - `¿Fuiste ...? / ¿Cuándo fuiste ...? / ¿Adónde fuiste?`
  - `... hace + tiempo` and `ya + fui/fuiste`

### 5 natural examples
1. Fui al banco hace una hora.
2. ¿Fuiste al supermercado ayer?
3. Ya fui a la farmacia.
4. ¿Cuándo fuiste al gimnasio?
5. No fui a la oficina el lunes.

## 3) pattern_mutation

Transform each base sentence as instructed.

1. Base: `Fui a la tienda.` -> turn into question (`tú`).
2. Base: `¿Fuiste al banco?` -> answer yes in full sentence.
3. Base: `Fui al cine.` -> add `yesterday`.
4. Base: `Ya fui al supermercado.` -> make it negative.
5. Base: `Fui a la playa.` -> change time to `two days ago`.
6. Base: `¿Cuándo fuiste a la farmacia?` -> answer `an hour ago`.
7. Base: `Fui a la biblioteca.` -> change destination to `la oficina`.
8. Base: `No fui al gimnasio.` -> make it affirmative.
9. Base: `Fui al restaurante hace un día.` -> change to `three days ago`.
10. Base: `¿Fuiste al hospital?` -> change to plural subject (`ustedes`).
11. Base: `Fui al aeropuerto.` -> make it a question with `cuándo`.
12. Base: `Ya fuiste a la tienda.` -> rewrite as proper question form.
13. Base: `Fui a la escuela el lunes.` -> change day to `martes`.
14. Base: `¿Adónde fuiste?` -> answer with `I went to the park`.
15. Base: `Fui al parque.` -> combine with contrast: `but not to the beach`.

## 4) contrastive_discrimination

Choose the best pattern in Spanish.

1. “I went to the store.” (`fui` vs `voy`)
2. “I go to the store (habit).” (`voy` vs `fui`)
3. “Did you go to the bank?” (`¿fuiste...?` vs `¿vas...?`) for yesterday.
4. “I already went.” (`ya fui` vs `fui ya`)
5. “I went two days ago.” (`hace dos días` vs `en dos días`)
6. “When did you go?” (`¿cuándo fuiste?` vs `¿cuándo vas?`) for past.
7. “Where did you go?” (`¿adónde fuiste?` vs `¿dónde ibas?`) finished action.
8. “I didn’t go.” (`no fui` vs `no voy`) past context.
9. “Did you already go to the office?” (`¿ya fuiste...?` vs `¿fuiste ya...?`)
10. “I went last week.” (`la semana pasada` vs `la semana próxima`)

## 7) common_errors

1. **Using `voy` for completed past trip**  
   Why: Present tense overused as default.  
   Correct: `Fui al banco ayer.`
2. **Using `en dos días` for “two days ago”**  
   Why: Direct preposition translation.  
   Correct: `Hace dos días.`
3. **Placing `ya` unnaturally at sentence end**  
   Why: English “already” position transfer.  
   Better: `Ya fui al supermercado.`
4. **Missing contraction `al`**  
   Why: Article+preposition not fused automatically.  
   Correct: `Fui al hospital.`
5. **Using `dónde` instead of `adónde` for movement**  
   Why: English uses one “where.”  
   Better here: `¿Adónde fuiste?`
6. **Forgetting inversion punctuation in questions**  
   Why: English punctuation habits.  
   Correct: `¿Fuiste a la tienda?`
7. **Using infinitive instead of conjugated preterite**  
   Why: Pattern overload from earlier lessons.  
   Correct: `Fui a la playa`, not `Ir a la playa`.
8. **Using present time markers with past form incorrectly**  
   Why: Mixed timeline in one sentence.  
   Correct: `Fui al cine anoche.`
9. **Confusing `ya fui` with `todavía no fui`**  
   Why: Already vs not yet contrast not internalized.  
   Correct: `Ya fui` / `Todavía no fui`
10. **Using `fuiste` with `yo` subject**  
    Why: Person ending confusion.  
    Correct: `Yo fui` / `Tú fuiste`
